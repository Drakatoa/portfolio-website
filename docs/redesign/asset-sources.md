## Trophy wordmark geometry font

`lib/trophies/barlow-condensed-black-italic.json` contains baked outlines from [Barlow Condensed Black Italic in Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/barlowcondensed). The SIL Open Font License is retained in `lib/trophies/Barlow-OFL.txt` and the JSON metadata. `scripts/build-trophy-font.py` converts the original font with FontTools; no runtime font-conversion dependency. The same Barlow family supplies webpage display text, keeping the 3D exhibits within the two-font palette.

Authored in `tools/trophy-studio/`; full provenance is in `tools/trophy-studio/docs/asset-inventory.md`.

Rajit should hold the pack in his own Unity Asset Store account.
- `public/trophies/014-eukarya.glb`: the Tiktaalik and its painted texture come from the Eukarya team repository `a-sriel/Eukarya` (committed by a-sriel). The repository has no licence file; credit the team.
- `lib/trophies/models/vectors/011-csa.js`: the P.F. Wang's emblem art is by Liz Michel; the shirt was designed with Chloe Tee and Liz Michel.
- The other logo vectors are potrace traces of each project's exported logo. They exclude third-party marks: the Emerson logo, the Olympic rings and the UTD monogram.
